FAQ

Do you place bids automatically?

No. Nothing on this website bids. A person bids only after you sign a written max-bid authorization and pay a deposit.

Why do I need a broker for Copart or IAA?

Many Copart/IAA lots (especially in some states, or salvage titles) can only be bought by licensed dealers. Brokers let the public buy those lots through their license. Public IAA accounts cost about $225/yr and can only bid on public lots.

What fees will I pay?

Hammer price + the auction's buyer fee (e.g. BaT/Cars & Bids 5% with $250 min / $7,500 max; Copart/IAA tiered fees plus gate/internet fees) + our broker fee (TBD) + transport + taxes/registration.

Are IAA/Copart cars damaged?

Often. They are mostly insurance total-loss, recovered-theft or fleet vehicles. Check damage type, title status (salvage/clean) and run status.

What does 'ex-rental' mean?

The vehicle was consigned by a rental or fleet company. IAA lists these under 'Rental Vehicles'; the exact company is not always shown publicly.

Is the rental marketplace live?

Not yet - it's a preview. We need commercial P2P car-sharing insurance and payment setup before accepting bookings.

How current are bids and end times?

The feed refreshes on a schedule (every 30 minutes planned). Always confirm on the source listing; auctions can extend in the final minutes.
