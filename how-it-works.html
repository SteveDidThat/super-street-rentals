About & how it works

Auction brokering

  1. Browse or request. Our feed aggregates public listings from enthusiast auctions (Bring a Trailer, PCARMARKET) and insurance/salvage auctions (IAA; Copart planned via broker/partner data).
  2. Inspect. We pull history reports and, where possible, arrange a third-party inspection.
  3. Authorize. You sign a max-bid authorization and pay a refundable deposit (Stripe - TBD).
  4. We bid. Copart and IAA restrict many lots to licensed dealers/brokers; we bid through our licensed account (licensing in progress). On public sites like BaT we can bid on your behalf or coach you.
  5. Pay & deliver. You pay the hammer price + the auction's buyer fees + our broker fee + transport. We handle title paperwork.

Peer-to-peer rentals

Owners list cars, renters request trips, hosts approve. Payments will run on Stripe Connect; renters will be ID-verified. Launching after insurance is secured.

Super Street Rentals is an independent business and is not affiliated with any auction house listed on this site.
